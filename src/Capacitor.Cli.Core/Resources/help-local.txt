kcap local — Run the local installation that `kcap setup --local` made

Usage: kcap local <subcommand> [options]

Subcommands:
  uninstall [--purge] [--force] [--yes]
                            Stop every kcap daemon and watcher and the local server, then remove the
                            server's service and binaries. Its data and identity are kept, so
                            `kcap setup --local` brings it back on the same URL.
      --purge               Also delete its data, logs, pending recordings and caches, and clear the
                            profile pointing at it. Its URL is retired: nothing is sent to it again,
                            and a new installation gets a new port.
      --force               Uninstall even while a daemon is running agents; they end with it.
      --yes, -y             Do not ask for confirmation.
  help                      Show this help

The installation lives in ~/.config/kcap/local, or in KCAP_LOCAL_ROOT when it is set.
